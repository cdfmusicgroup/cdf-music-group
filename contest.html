The Fun Part

How To Enter

No entry fee. No purchase. Just your music. Post one original song, tag CDF, and send us the link.

1

Record One Original Song, Live

Perform one original song you wrote, captured live. No produced music videos and no lip syncing. Phone footage is completely fine. Include as much of the song as you can fit within the Reels length limit. A full song is ideal, and a continuous portion of at least 60 seconds is fine if your song runs long.

2

Post It And Tag CDF

Post your performance to your own feed as a Reel or video. In the caption, include the required tag and hashtag so we can find and count your entry.

Send Us The Link

Share The Announcement

Share the CDF contest announcement to your story when you enter. This step is required to complete your entry.

The Boring But Important Part

Official Rules

These are the official rules of the CDF Independent Artist Contest, administered solely by CDF Music Group. By entering, you agree to them.

01Administrator

This contest is run and administered solely by CDF Music Group, Illinois. Prizes for this contest are provided by donating sponsors. Donating sponsors are not administrators of the contest, are not responsible for running it, and have no liability in connection with it. All questions, entries, and claims are directed to CDF Music Group at contest@cdfmusicgroup.com.

02Eligibility

Entrants must be at least 18 years old at the time of entry. The contest is open to legal residents of the United States only. Employees of CDF Music Group and their immediate families are not eligible. Void where prohibited.

03Entry Period

The contest begins on September 1, 2026 and ends on September 30, 2026 at midnight CST. Entries received outside this window will not be considered. Proof of posting or emailing is not proof of receipt.

04How To Enter

To enter, an entrant must complete all of the following during the entry period:

  • Post a video of one original song performed live to their own public social account.
  • Include the caption tags @cdfmusicgroup and @amuse.io and the hashtag #CDFMusicContest.
  • Share the CDF contest announcement to their story.
  • Email the link to the posted video to contest@cdfmusicgroup.com.

Limit one entry per person. No entry fee and no purchase of any kind is necessary to enter or win. A purchase will not improve your chances of winning.

05Performance And Content Requirements

The submitted song must be original and written by the entrant. The performance must be live. Produced music videos, lip syncing, and pre recorded studio tracks played over the video are not permitted. Entrants should include as much of the song as they can within the Reels length limit, with a continuous portion of at least 60 seconds. CDF Music Group reserves the right to disqualify any entry that is not original, contains offensive or unlawful material, or does not follow these rules.

06Email And Marketing Consent

By emailing your entry to contest@cdfmusicgroup.com, you consent to receive email communications from CDF Music Group, including updates about the contest and future news, sessions, and opportunities. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any of our emails.

07Rights And Ownership

You keep your music. Entering does not transfer ownership of your song or your performance to CDF Music Group. You retain all rights to your work.

By entering, you grant CDF Music Group a limited, non exclusive permission to display, repost, and use your submitted performance in connection with promoting and administering this contest across CDF channels. For the two winners, this permission also covers the collaborative Reel and feature described in the prizes.

By entering, you confirm that you own or control all rights to the music and the performance you submit, and that your entry does not infringe on anyone else's rights.

08Judging And Winner Selection

CDF Music Group will select finalists from all valid entries and announce them on October 7, 2026. All finalist videos will be posted together on the same day so that every finalist has the same voting window. There are two winners, selected as follows:

  • People's Choice Winner. Selected by a public vote open from October 7 to October 13, 2026.
  • CDF Pick Winner. Selected by the CDF Music Group team based on songwriting, performance quality, and originality.

The same artist cannot win both categories. If the same artist would win both, that artist receives the CDF Pick and the People's Choice passes to the next eligible finalist. In the event of a tie, CDF Music Group will make the final determination.

09Prizes

The People's Choice Winner receives one year of amuse Artist Plus distribution and a collaborative feature Reel shared on CDF socials.

The CDF Pick Winner receives their song posted as a collaborative Reel by CDF Music Group and amuse, two years of amuse Professional distribution, a Shure MV7+ microphone, custom branded guitar picks from InTune Guitar Picks, and a CDF studio session and interview. The studio session and interview are offered in person if the winner is within reasonable driving distance of the studio, or as a remote interview otherwise, at the sole discretion of CDF Music Group. Terms and conditions apply to the session and interview.

Prizes are non transferable and cannot be exchanged for cash. Any tax on a prize is the responsibility of the winner. Prizes are provided by donating sponsors, who bear no liability for the prizes or the contest.

10Winner Notification

Winners will be notified by email or direct message on or about October 14, 2026. If a winner cannot be reached within 15 days, or does not meet the eligibility requirements, that prize may be forfeited and an alternate winner may be selected at the discretion of CDF Music Group.

11CDF Sole Discretion

CDF Music Group reserves the right, at its sole discretion, to modify, suspend, extend, or cancel this contest at any time and for any reason. CDF Music Group also reserves the right to decline to name a winner in either category, to disqualify any entry, and to make any final decision regarding the contest. All decisions of CDF Music Group are final and binding.

12General Conditions

By entering, entrants agree to release CDF Music Group and its donating sponsors from any and all claims arising out of participation in the contest or receipt, use, or misuse of any prize.

This promotion is in no way sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Instagram, Meta, TikTok, YouTube, or any other platform. By entering, you release these platforms from any responsibility.

No purchase necessary. Void where prohibited.
